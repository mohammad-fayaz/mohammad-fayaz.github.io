## Abstract

**Background:**  
In recent years, increasing attention has been directed toward understanding the mechanistic basis of traditional medical systems and their relationship with modern biomedical sciences. Metabolomics has emerged as a promising approach to characterize systemic metabolic changes and may provide a bridge between Persian Medicine and modern biomedical frameworks.

**Materials and Methods:**  
This review study conducted an advanced literature search of English-language publications from 2003 to 2020 using databases including PubMed, Medline, ScienceDirect, Cochrane, and Embase. Keywords included “traditional medicine,” “temperament,” and “metabolomics.”

**Results:**  
Previous studies, particularly in Traditional Chinese Medicine, have demonstrated that metabolomic profiles can distinguish disease subtypes based on traditional diagnostic frameworks. In Persian Medicine, limited studies have investigated metabolomic or proteomic variations associated with different Mizaj (temperament) types.

**Conclusion:**  
Metabolomics may provide a novel framework for understanding core concepts of Persian Medicine, including Mizaj classification and therapeutic mechanisms of traditional formulations. However, further systematic research is required, particularly in human populations, to integrate traditional medicine with modern omics-based approaches.
