## Abstract
This study investigated the application of parametric and nonparametric optimization methods for wool dyeing processes. The preparation and production of multilamellar liposomes from soya lecithin containing 75% phosphatidylcholine were carried out, and the behavior of liposomes in dye baths was evaluated under different experimental conditions, including temperature, time, sodium sulphate concentration, pH, and dye concentration. Using a Box–Behnken design within the framework of Response Surface Methodology (RSM), the performance of parametric and nonparametric optimization approaches was compared. The results demonstrated that when parametric models do not adequately fit the data, nonparametric methods can serve as effective alternative optimization techniques.
