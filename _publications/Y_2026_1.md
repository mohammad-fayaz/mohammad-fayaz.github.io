## Abstract

### Introduction
EEGLAB is a widely used open-source software for EEG data analysis with over two decades of global application. This study provides a bibliometric assessment of EEGLAB-related publications in Scopus and Web of Science indexed sources across Asia-Pacific and Arabian regions.

### Methods
Bibliometric analysis was conducted using the *Bibliometrix* R package. The dataset included publications from 80 countries across ESCAP and ESCWA regions, covering 58 Asia-Pacific and 22 Arabian countries. Multiple bibliometric laws and network analyses (Lotka’s law, Bradford’s law, co-citation networks, and historiographic mapping) were applied.

### Results
EEGLAB’s primary publication has accumulated over 22,000 citations globally. China, Australia, Japan, and South Korea were among the top contributing countries. Regional analysis showed that ESCAP and ESCWA countries contribute a substantial share of publications, though citation impact remains below global average levels. Emerging research themes include deep learning, emotion recognition, schizophrenia detection, and cognitive neuroscience applications.

### Conclusion
The study highlights growing global and regional contributions to EEGLAB-based research. However, disparities in citation impact and collaboration intensity suggest the need for stronger international research integration.
