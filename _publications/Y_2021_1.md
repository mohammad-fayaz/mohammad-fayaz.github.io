## Title

Wet-Cupping on Calf Muscles in Polycystic Ovary Syndrome: A Quasi-Experimental Study

## Authors

## Abstract

**Objectives**: Current modalities for treating polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) are not curative and long-term use may cause adverse effects. Based on Persian Medicine, wet cupping on calf muscles may induce menstrual bleeding. This study evaluated its effect on menstrual cycle regulation, hirsutism, and metabolic markers in PCOS patients.
**Methods**: A quasi-experimental study was conducted on 66 women aged 20–40 years with PCOS and menstrual irregularity. Participants underwent wet cupping on calf muscles and were followed for 12 weeks. Outcomes included menstrual frequency, hirsutism score, insulin resistance (IR), and free androgen index (FAI).
**Results**: Wet cupping significantly improved menstrual frequency and reduced hirsutism (p < 0.001). No significant changes were observed in IR or FAI. Mild transient side effects were reported in a minority of participants.
**Conclusion**: Wet cupping may be considered a complementary option for PCOS management in selected patients.
