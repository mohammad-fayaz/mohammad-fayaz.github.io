## Abstract (English)

**Background:**  
Text mining methods have been widely used to analyze religious and literary texts, including the Quran, Bible, and classical Persian and Western literature. However, limited computational analysis has been conducted on al-Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya.

**Methods:**  
All 54 supplications of al-Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya were extracted and preprocessed using R. Word frequency analysis, word cloud visualization, clustering, and text network analysis were applied.

**Results:**  
A total of 15,881 words were identified. The most frequent words included “Allahomma,” “Muhammad,” “Allah,” “Sal,” and “Kol.” Word cloud visualizations and clustering analyses revealed recurring linguistic structures and thematic patterns across prayers.

**Conclusion:**  
This study provides a preliminary computational exploration of al-Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya and demonstrates the potential of text mining methods for analyzing religious texts. It serves as a foundation for future advanced computational and linguistic studies.
