## Teaching Focus

Courses emphasize the application of statistical and computational methods in medical and health sciences, including:

- Descriptive and inferential statistics  
- Medical research methodology  
- Data analysis using SPSS and modern tools  
- Introduction to artificial intelligence in health sciences
