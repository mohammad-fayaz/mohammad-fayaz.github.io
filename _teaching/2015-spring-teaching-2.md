## Teaching Focus

Courses emphasize the application of statistical and computational methods in non-medical fields, including:

- Statistical inference and modeling  
- Econometrics and applied statistics  
- Data analysis using R, Python, and statistical software  
- Research methodology in social sciences and economics  
- Introduction to data science and computational tools
