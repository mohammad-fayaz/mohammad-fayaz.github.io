## Abstract

Statistical analysis of EEG-FMRI data in neuroscience presents major theoretical and practical challenges, including complex dependencies, high-dimensional structure, sparsity, and smoothing issues.

To address these challenges, many Functional Data Analysis (FDA) approaches have been developed, where data are represented as functions or curves rather than discrete observations. These methods rely on different basis expansions and modeling frameworks.

This poster provides a survey of recent statistical methods for EEG-FMRI analysis, with a particular focus on Functional Data Analysis approaches and their applications in neuroscience. The aim is to summarize methodological developments and highlight their strengths and limitations in modeling complex neuroimaging data.
